📚 Classes Homework Series

Why three parts? Classes are the trickiest concept in this term. Spreading them across 3 short homeworks (one after each Classes lesson) gives your brain time to absorb each idea before the next one stacks on top. Do them in order.
🧱

HW01 — Classes Foundations (after L03)

After L03 · 30–40 min · /40 marks

L04 (next lesson) introduces inheritance — that only makes sense if your class basics are rock-solid. This homework checks you can read, write, and debug a simple class.
⚡

HW02 — Inheritance Power-Up (after L04)

After L04 · 35–45 min · /40 marks

L05 (next lesson) puts your classes to work in a real game system — heroes vs goblins. Before that you need to be confident with inheritance: parent classes, child classes, super(), and overriding methods.
⚔️

HW03 — Classes in Systems (after L05)

After L05 · 40–50 min · /40 marks

Final classes homework before you build adventure_v1.py. This one stretches you: methods that change other objects, lists of instances, and combat loops. Get this right and adventure_v1 will feel easy.
How marks work: Each homework is /40, self-marked. Multiple choice + fill-in are auto-graded. Build tasks (Q11) are checked by Ms Gao in class. The point is learning, not the score — but doing them honestly is the single best predictor of how easy adventure_v1.py will feel.
