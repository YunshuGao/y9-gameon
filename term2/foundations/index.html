For students who haven't finished Grok Academy — Python Intro 1. These three worksheets pair video lessons with practice exercises. Work through them in order. Each one auto-saves on your laptop. You don't need to do them all today — pace yourself.

Foundations 01 — Why programming?

Foundations 03 — Writing paragraphs of code
